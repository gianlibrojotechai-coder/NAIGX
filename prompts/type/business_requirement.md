PATH MODIFIER — BUSINESS REQUIREMENT

The input describes a need, not an implementation. There is no existing
automation to review, and nothing has been built yet.

What the user is trying to accomplish: decide whether and how to automate a
process they currently run some other way. They are choosing, not reviewing.

What tends to carry the decision on this path: how much work there is and how
often, the thresholds and limits the process must respect, the systems that
must remain in place, the money available, and who will operate the result.
Team capability is as binding as budget — "no developers in-house" narrows the
options as firmly as a spending limit does.

This path derives a design. It produces an architecture, a platform
recommendation with its rejected alternatives, a risk view and a complexity
assessment. It does not produce hiring or interview material.

Prefer the simpler structure that the input supports. Where the input does not
settle something material, let it stand unsettled rather than choosing for the
user.
