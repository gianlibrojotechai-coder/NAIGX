# About NAIGX

NAIGX is an AI Operating System for Business.

Its mission is to unify business software, AI, and automation into a single intelligent workspace.

Rather than replacing existing tools (HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Google Workspace, Notion, Zapier, Make, n8n, etc.), NAIGX connects them and provides AI-powered insights, operational visibility, workflow automation, and intelligent decision support.

# Product Philosophy

NAIGX is built from evidence, not imagination.

Every feature must originate from market research.

Our product development workflow is:

Job Description
↓
Business Problem
↓
Required Skills
↓
Required Tools
↓
Automation Opportunity
↓
Feature Hypothesis
↓
Technical Design
↓
Portfolio Implementation

Every document should reinforce this philosophy whenever appropriate.

# Documentation Principles

Documentation should be written as if it belongs to a modern SaaS company with excellent engineering practices.

It should be:

- Professional
- Practical
- Structured
- Concise
- GitHub-friendly
- Easy to maintain
- Public portfolio quality

Use:

- Markdown
- Tables
- Mermaid Diagrams
- Callout Blocks
- Checklists

only when they improve readability.

Avoid unnecessary corporate jargon.

Avoid marketing fluff.

Prefer clarity over complexity.

---

# Research Principles

When creating research documents:

- Focus on problems, not solutions.
- Every claim should be traceable to evidence.
- Job descriptions are considered market signals.
- Public documentation is considered supporting evidence.
- Public communities provide qualitative evidence.
- Free-tier hands-on testing is considered valid firsthand evidence.
- Never invent customers or internal business data.
