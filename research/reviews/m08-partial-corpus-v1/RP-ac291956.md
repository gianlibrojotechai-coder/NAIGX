## 1 · The submitted input

```text
I'd like to automate our annual insurance renewal review.

Once a year, in October, our office manager pulls together our current policies
(employers' liability, public liability, contents, and professional indemnity),
gets renewal quotes from our broker, and sits down with the two directors for
about an hour to decide whether to renew, switch, or adjust cover levels.

The broker sends everything as PDFs by email. The office manager puts the key
numbers into a spreadsheet so the directors can compare year on year - premium,
excess, cover limit, and any exclusions that changed. That takes maybe 90 minutes.
Then the meeting happens, a decision is made, and the office manager emails the
broker to confirm.

It's four policies, one broker, once a year. But it feels manual and I keep
reading that we should be automating this kind of admin, so I wanted to ask what
an automated version would look like.

We use Google Workspace and Xero. No other systems really.
```
