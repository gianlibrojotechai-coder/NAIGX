## 1 · The submitted input

```text
Patient referral intake automation - requirements

Background

We are a private diagnostic imaging provider with six clinics in the north of
England. Referrals reach us from three sources: NHS trusts via the national
e-Referral Service, private GPs who email or fax a referral letter, and self-pay
patients who book through our website.

Volumes last year were 31,400 referrals: about 62% e-Referral, 26% private GP,
and 12% self-pay. Referral volume is growing roughly 15% year on year.

Current process

A team of five referral coordinators works through a shared Outlook mailbox. For
each referral they: read the letter, identify the referring clinician, extract
patient demographics and NHS number, determine which scan is being requested,
check whether the requested scan matches our clinical protocols, check insurer or
self-pay status, and then create the record in our RIS (Radiology Information
System, which is Soliton) and book an appointment slot.

Coordinators handle around 30 referrals per person per day. The e-Referral ones are
structured and take about four minutes. The emailed GP letters are unstructured PDFs
or scans and take twelve to twenty minutes, sometimes longer if handwriting is
involved or the NHS number is missing and has to be looked up.

What we need

We want the structured e-Referral intake automated end to end - matched to a
patient record, protocol-checked, and booked without a coordinator touching it,
with anything failing a check routed to a human queue with the reason attached.

For the unstructured GP letters we want the extraction done automatically and
presented to a coordinator for confirmation rather than booked directly. We are not
comfortable with unattended booking from a document we have not validated.

Constraints

Soliton exposes an HL7 v2 interface and a limited REST API added in 2023. The REST
API covers patient lookup and appointment booking but not protocol rules, which are
held in a separate configuration table our RIS vendor manages.

Patient data must remain within the UK. We are subject to UK GDPR and NHS Digital's
Data Security and Protection Toolkit. Any processor must be covered by a data
processing agreement, and we cannot send identifiable patient data to a service
without one.

We have an internal development capability of two people, both of whom are already
committed to a patient portal project until at least the end of the year.

Success would be coordinators spending their time on the exception queue rather than
on structured referrals, and same-day booking for e-Referral intake instead of the
current one-to-two working day lag.
```
