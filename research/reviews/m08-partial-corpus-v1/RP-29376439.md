## 1 · The submitted input

```text
Integration Engineer, Clinical Data — Contract (W2), 12 months, fully remote (US)

$85–$105/hr depending on experience. Must be authorized to work in the US; we cannot
sponsor for this engagement.

Company

Series C digital health company. Our platform coordinates care for patients with
chronic conditions, and we exchange data with health systems, labs, and payers.
Around 400 employees.

The engagement

We are consolidating how clinical data enters our platform. Today we have twelve
distinct intake paths built over four years by three different teams — a mix of HL7
v2 feeds, FHIR R4 APIs, SFTP drops of CSV, and two vendors who send us PDFs. Each
path has its own transformation logic and its own error handling, and there is no
common view of what failed.

You would be the engineer on a two-person team consolidating this into one intake
pipeline. The other person is our clinical informaticist, who owns the data model
and will not be writing code.

Requirements

- 5+ years integration engineering, including at least two years with healthcare
  data standards
- Direct experience with HL7 v2 and FHIR. We will ask you specific questions about
  segment mapping and resource references
- Experience operating under HIPAA, including what you can and cannot log
- Comfortable owning production on-call for the pipeline you build
- Able to work with a clinical SME who is not technical, and translate in both
  directions

Preferred

- Prior work with a health information exchange
- Terminology mapping experience (LOINC, SNOMED)
- AWS; we run on it but will not disqualify on cloud

Process and terms

Screening call, a 90-minute technical conversation with our principal engineer, and
a conversation with the informaticist. No take-home. Start date is flexible within
six weeks. Extension is possible but not promised — we would rather be honest about
that than imply otherwise.
```
