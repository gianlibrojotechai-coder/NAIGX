## 1 · The submitted input

```text
Quote turnaround is killing us and I want to fix it properly this time.

We manufacture bespoke steel fabrications. A quote request arrives by email with
drawings attached, usually PDF but sometimes DWG. An estimator opens the drawing,
works out material volumes, looks up current steel prices in a spreadsheet our
buyer maintains, adds labour hours from a rate card, applies a margin, and writes
the quote in Word from a template. Then it goes to a director for approval if it's
over £25,000.

We do about 60 quote requests a month. Turnaround averages six working days and
we've lost at least four jobs this year to competitors who came back inside two.

I've tried to solve this twice. Once with a quoting module our ERP vendor sold us,
which we abandoned because it couldn't handle bespoke work - it assumes a catalogue.
And once by hiring a second estimator, which helped for about four months until
volume grew again.

So I'm not looking for another tool recommendation on its own. I want to understand
where the six days actually goes, which parts of that are the estimator's judgement
and which parts are just data being moved around by hand, and then what could
realistically be automated given that the drawing interpretation genuinely does need
an experienced person.

Systems: Microsoft 365, Sage 200 for the ERP, and the buyer's price spreadsheet
which lives on SharePoint and gets updated weekly.
```
