## 1 · The submitted input

```text
We need to automate our supplier invoice approval process.

Right now invoices arrive as PDF attachments to accounts@ourcompany.com. Someone
in finance downloads each one, keys the supplier name, invoice number, date, line
items and total into our accounting system (Xero), then emails the relevant
department head for approval. Once the department head replies "approved", finance
marks it approved in Xero and schedules payment on the next payment run.

Volumes: roughly 400-500 invoices per month, from about 90 active suppliers.
Around 60% come from the same 15 suppliers with consistent formats.

Current problems:
- Data entry takes one person roughly 3 days per week.
- Approval emails get lost in inboxes. We have no view of what is waiting on whom.
- We miss early payment discounts (2% on about a third of our suppliers) because
  approval takes 5-9 days on average when the discount window is 10 days.
- Duplicate invoices have been paid twice on three occasions in the last year.

What we want: invoices captured automatically, routed to the right approver based
on department and amount, with reminders when someone sits on an approval, and a
dashboard showing what is pending. Approvals over £10,000 need the finance director
as a second approver. Anything over £50,000 needs two directors.

Constraints: we must keep Xero as the system of record - our accountant requires it.
We are a UK company, so we need to handle UK VAT correctly on the captured data.
Budget is up to about £400/month for tooling. We have no developers in-house; our
IT is one person who is comfortable with tools like Zapier but does not write code.
```
